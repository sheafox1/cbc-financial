How It Works

From your numbers to your closing

A lender that has built projects of its own, so the process is built around the builder.

1. Run your numbers

Use our Quick Flip tool to check profit margin, loan to value and carry cost, then apply with the results.

2. We vet the deal

Our budget, spec and roadblock checks look for missed costs and zoning or municipal surprises.

3. Close fast

Fast responses and closings in as few as 10 days, from a team whose full time job is construction lending.

4. Build with a partner

We act as a consultant through the project, with insight on fit and finish and the local market.
